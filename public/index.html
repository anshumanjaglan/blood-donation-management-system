How the Donation Process Works

Simple, safe, and takes less than 30 minutes from start to finish

01

Registration

Sign up online with your basic contact and medical details.

02

Quick Health Check

Hemoglobin check, blood pressure & temperature screening.

03

Donation (10 Mins)

Relax in a sterile medical recliner while giving 1 unit (350-450 ml).

04

Rest & Refresh

Enjoy snacks and juices while resting for 10-15 minutes.
